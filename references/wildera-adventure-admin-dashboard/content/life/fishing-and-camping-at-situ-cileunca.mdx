- **Activity**: Lakeside dome camping, sunset & morning fishing, campfire conversations
- **Timing**: February 2024
- **Crew**: 5 High School Friends (SMA reunion)

---

## 1. The Call of Pangalengan: Breaking the Routine

As we transition deeper into our twenties, maintaining high school friendships gets noticeably harder. Group chats go quiet for weeks, plans to meet get delayed by conflicting work deadlines, and promises of *"nanti kita ngumpul ya"* often end up hanging indefinitely.

One evening, someone finally decided to break the cycle: *"Yuk kita camping sama mancing di Situ Cileunca. Bawa tenda, bawa joran, kita cari angin dingin."*

Situated approximately 45 kilometers south of Bandung at an elevation of 1,550 meters above sea level, **Situ Cileunca** in Pangalengan is famous for its cool plateau climate, vast tea plantations, and tranquil artificial lake created during the Dutch colonial period. It offered the ideal getaway: far enough to disconnect from city noise, yet accessible enough for a spontaneous weekend trip.

![Walking down the scenic dirt trail through highland vegetable plantations towards our lakeside camping ground.](/images/situ-cileunca/situ-cileunca-trail-plantation.webp)

Leaving the highway and climbing the winding mountain pass, the humid air of the lowlands was replaced by crisp, pine-scented breezes. We parked near the plantation slopes and carried our gear down the earthen footpaths lined with vibrant cabbage fields toward the water's edge.

---

## 2. Casting Lines into Twilight

We pitched our tents on the grassy embankment directly fronting the lake. With our portable camp chairs unfolded and the water lapping gently against the shore, the rush of the week immediately melted away.

Situ Cileunca is well known among freshwater anglers for tilapia (*ikan nila*), carp (*ikan mas*), and freshwater gourami. We unpacked our tackle boxes, tied on our hooks and floats, baited the rigs with simple fish meal, and cast our lines into the calm surface.

![Quiet focus at the shoreline as dusk settles over the water, watching the float drift on gentle ripples.](/images/situ-cileunca/situ-cileunca-fishing-shore.webp)

There is something inherently therapeutic about freshwater fishing. You cannot rush it. In a world that constantly rewards immediate notifications and rapid turnarounds, sitting still on a folding chair watching a bobber teach you the value of patient observation.

![Watching the golden twilight sky paint reflections over Situ Cileunca as evening settles over Pangalengan.](/images/situ-cileunca/situ-cileunca-fishing-twilight.webp)

As the sun sank behind the surrounding mountain ridges, the sky shifted from pale blue into violet and dusky amber. The lake mirrored the twilight like polished glass, broken only when a curious fish nibbled at the bait.

---

## 3. Fairy Lights, Cold Mist, & Unfiltered Nostalgia

When night fell across Pangalengan, the mountain chill set in rapidly, dropping temperatures down to around 16°C. The campsite came alive with rows of warm fairy lights strung between the trees, casting a golden glow over our dome tents.

![Our campsite under the stars, framed by warm fairy lights and the dark highland woods.](/images/situ-cileunca/situ-cileunca-tent-night.webp)

We bundled up in hoodies, windbreakers, and beanies. A small camp stove hissed quietly as we boiled water for black coffee, sweet ginger tea, and the undisputed king of Indonesian camping food: steaming instant noodles with extra chili.

Sitting around the circle outside the tents, the conversation flowed naturally. With high school friends, there is zero need to impress anyone. There are no professional titles or corporate jargon. We laughed endlessly over ridiculous classroom memories, the teachers we drove crazy, silly teenage antics, and how each of our paths has diverged since graduation.

Those unfiltered hours under the open night sky were the true highlight of the weekend—a reminder that despite how much life changes, the core camaraderie remains completely intact.

---

## 4. Morning on the Water: Wooden Boats & the Pontoon Dock

We woke up early to mist hovering softly across the surface of the lake. The air was crisp and invigorating. Across the water, a local boatman wearing an orange life vest rowed a traditional wooden longboat, moving with practiced, rhythmic strokes against the backdrop of rolling green hills.

![A local boatman rowing a traditional wooden canoe across the mirror-like morning waters of Situ Cileunca.](/images/situ-cileunca/situ-cileunca-wooden-boat.webp)

For our morning fishing session, we relocated to a floating modular pontoon dock positioned further out in the deeper section of the lake.

![Casting lines from the floating pontoon dock with panoramic views of the Pangalengan mountain ridge.](/images/situ-cileunca/situ-cileunca-floating-dock-fishing.webp)

Setting our camp chairs directly on the floating blocks, we had an unobstructed 360-degree panorama of Situ Cileunca. The clouds drifted across the southern mountain range, and the sun began warming the water.

![Banter, tangled lines, and genuine laughs on the floating dock.](/images/situ-cileunca/situ-cileunca-friends-dock-laughing.webp)

Naturally, our "serious" fishing quickly turned into an hour of pure banter. Between someone nearly dropping their sandals into the lake, untangling bird-nested monofilament line, and celebrating every minor bite like a trophy catch, the dock was filled with nonstop laughter.

---

## 5. Brotherhood That Stands the Test of Time

By midday, the high mountain sun warned us it was time to pack up our tents and gear before the afternoon rain typical of the Bandung highlands rolled in.

Before loading the cars, we took a couple of group photos to commemorate the reunion.

![The crew standing proud next to our orange dome tent after a memorable night by the lake.](/images/situ-cileunca/situ-cileunca-group-tents.webp)

![All five of us in the open grassy field under the eucalyptus trees, ready to head back to the city.](/images/situ-cileunca/situ-cileunca-group-field.webp)

As adulthood pulls everyone in different directions with career obligations and personal milestones, it’s easy to let old connections fade into nostalgia. But moments like this prove that real friendships don't expire with time. All it takes is a weekend, a quiet lake in Pangalengan, and a few folding chairs to pick up right where you left off.
